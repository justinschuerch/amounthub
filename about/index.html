About AmountHub

AmountHub is a growing collection of simple online calculators built to give useful answers without unnecessary complexity.

What AmountHub is for

The goal is straightforward: make practical calculations quick and easy to understand. Each calculator is designed around a specific task, with clear inputs, useful unit conversions and an explanation of how the result is estimated.

The site currently focuses on construction and material calculators, starting with gravel and concrete, with more categories planned over time.

How calculations are handled

Calculator inputs are processed directly in your browser. Results are intended as planning estimates and may differ from supplier specifications, site conditions or professional calculations.

Why the site exists

AmountHub is an independent project built around a simple idea: useful tools should be fast to reach, easy to use and available without requiring an account.

Questions or feedback

If you spot an issue with a calculator or have a suggestion for a useful tool, you can contact hello@amounthub.com.
