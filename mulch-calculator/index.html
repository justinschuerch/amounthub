How much mulch do I need?

Mulch is usually sold by volume. Measure the bed length and width, then decide how deep the finished layer should be. The calculator multiplies those values to estimate cubic metres or cubic yards.

Unlike stone or sand, mulch is light and often sold in bags, so this page focuses on volume and bag count rather than weight.

Choosing mulch depth

A shallow layer may be enough to refresh the appearance of an existing bed, while a new area usually needs more material. Very deep mulch can hold excess moisture against plant stems, so the ideal depth depends on the plants, soil and mulch type.

Mulch example

A bed that measures 6 m × 2 m with a 7 cm mulch layer needs about 0.84 m³, or roughly 17 bags when using 50-litre bags.

Bags versus bulk mulch

Bagged mulch is convenient for small beds and easy to transport. Large landscape jobs are often cheaper and faster with bulk material. Use the cubic-volume result when comparing delivery quotes, and the bag result when buying packaged mulch.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for bark chips?

Yes. The volume calculation works for bark, wood chips and similar loose landscape coverings.

Why does fresh mulch seem to shrink?

Pieces settle into gaps and organic material can break down over time, so a layer may become thinner after installation.

Can I calculate several beds?

Calculate each bed separately and add the volumes, especially if the beds have different depths.
