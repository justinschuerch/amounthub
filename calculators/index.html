How AmountHub calculators are built

Each calculator is designed around a specific task rather than using one generic formula for every page. Material calculators may use volume and density, while finish-material calculators use coverage, unit size, pack size or waste allowances. Results are planning estimates and should be checked against supplier specifications when purchasing materials.
