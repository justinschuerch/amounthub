How much flooring do I need?

Start with the floor area: multiply room length by room width. Flooring is normally purchased by coverage rather than by individual plank count, so the calculator adds your waste allowance to the room area and compares the result with the coverage printed on each pack.

Because stores sell complete packs, the result is rounded up to the next full pack.

Why allow extra flooring?

Planks and boards must be cut at walls, doorways and around fixed objects. Pattern direction and room shape affect how much of each offcut can be reused. A straightforward rectangular room may need less spare material than several connected spaces or a diagonal installation.

Keeping leftover boards can also be useful if a damaged plank needs to be replaced later.

Flooring calculation example

A 5 m × 4 m room covers 20 m². With 10% waste you should plan for 22 m². If each pack covers 2.2 m², the estimate is 10 packs.

What this calculator does not include

The result covers the finish flooring only. Underlay, moisture barriers, trims, thresholds, adhesive and skirting are separate products and may follow different measurement rules. Always check the installation instructions for the specific flooring system.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for laminate flooring?

Yes. Enter the pack coverage shown on the laminate packaging.

What about irregular rooms?

Split the room into simple rectangles, calculate each area, add them together, then apply the waste allowance.

Does it work for vinyl planks?

Yes, as long as the product is sold with a known coverage per pack.
