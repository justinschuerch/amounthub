How much sand do I need?

Sand quantities are usually based on the volume of the space you want to fill. Measure the length and width of the area, then choose the planned sand depth. The calculator converts those dimensions to one common unit before multiplying them together.

For a rectangular area, the basic formula is length × width × depth = sand volume. A weight estimate is then calculated using the selected sand density.

Why sand weight changes

A cubic metre of sand does not have one fixed weight. Fine grains pack differently from coarse grains, and wet sand can be noticeably heavier than dry material because water fills the spaces between particles. This calculator therefore offers several typical density assumptions rather than presenting one number as exact.

If a supplier gives a bulk density for the exact product you plan to buy, use the supplier figure when ordering.

Sand calculation example

A paving bed that is 5 m long, 3 m wide and 5 cm deep has a theoretical volume of 0.75 m³. With 10% extra, the planning quantity becomes about 0.83 m³.

Common uses for this calculator

You can use the tool for bedding sand below pavers, levelling layers, playground or sandbox fill, landscaping projects and other rectangular areas. Different applications may need different sand grades and compaction methods, so the calculated quantity does not replace project specifications.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should paving sand be?

The required depth depends on the paving system and local installation guidance. Measure the finished compacted layer you intend to build rather than guessing from the loose material pile.

Does the calculator include waste?

Yes. The default allowance is 10%, and you can change it before calculating.

Can I use feet and inches?

Yes. Switch to Imperial to enter feet or inches and see volume in cubic yards and weight in US tons and pounds.
