How much concrete do I need?

For a rectangular slab, multiply the length by the width by the thickness to find the concrete volume. This calculator converts your measurements into cubic metres or cubic yards and can add a percentage for waste and small variations.

The basic formula is Length × Width × Thickness = Concrete Volume.

Should I order extra concrete?

A small allowance is commonly useful because excavations and formwork are rarely perfectly exact. The calculator starts with a 10% allowance, but you can change it to suit your project.

Concrete calculation example

A slab measuring 5 m × 3 m × 10 cm has a base volume of 1.5 m³.

With a 10% allowance, the estimated order quantity is 1.65 m³.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for a patio or shed base?

Yes. It works for rectangular slabs such as patios, shed bases, paths and similar projects.

Does it support feet and inches?

Yes. Switch to Imperial to enter feet or inches and receive results in cubic yards and cubic feet.

Can I estimate the concrete cost?

Yes. Enter a price per cubic metre or cubic yard and the calculator will estimate the material cost.
