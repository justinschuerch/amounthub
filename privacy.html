Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

AmountHub provides free online calculators and informational content. This privacy policy explains how information may be processed when you visit amounthub.com.

Information you enter into calculators

Calculations on AmountHub are performed directly in your web browser. Values entered into calculators, such as measurements, material choices and prices, are not sent to an AmountHub server or stored by AmountHub.

Cookies and advertising

AmountHub uses Google AdSense for advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, personalise and measure advertisements where permitted by law and by your consent choices.

Visitors in regions where consent is required may be shown a consent message that allows them to accept, reject or manage advertising and privacy choices.

Google AdSense

Google may process information about visits to this website to provide advertising services, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and improve its services. Google's use of information is governed by Google's own privacy terms and policies.

Hosting and technical data

AmountHub is hosted using GitHub Pages. Infrastructure providers may process technical information required to deliver the website, such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs.

Third-party services

AmountHub may use services such as Google AdSense and Google Search Console. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact and corrections

For privacy-related questions, contact privacy@amounthub.com. General calculator feedback can also be sent through the contact page.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the website, advertising setup, third-party services or applicable privacy requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
