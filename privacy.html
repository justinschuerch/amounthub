Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

AmountHub provides free online calculators and informational content. This privacy policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit amounthub.com.

Calculations on AmountHub are performed directly in your web browser. Values entered into the gravel calculator, such as measurements, material choices and prices, are not sent to an AmountHub server or stored by AmountHub.

AmountHub uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, personalise and measure advertisements where permitted by law and by your consent choices.

Visitors in regions where consent is required may be shown a consent message that allows them to accept, reject or manage advertising and privacy choices.

Google AdSense

Google may collect information about visits to this website in order to provide advertising services, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and improve its services.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services by visiting Google's privacy documentation.

Server and technical data

AmountHub is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most web hosting services, infrastructure providers may process technical information required to deliver the website, such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs.

Third-party services

AmountHub may use third-party services such as Google AdSense and Google Search Console. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the website, advertising setup or applicable privacy requirements change.
